## ro-crate-masp

The MASP profile validator is a Python port of the validator in [ro-crate-masp](https://github.com/Language-Research-Technology/ro-crate-masp) by the Language Data Commons of Australia (Language Research Technology), licensed under Apache-2.0. Its test suite and example crates were used to check that the port agrees with the original.
